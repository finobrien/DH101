## Reflection
AI futures are mostly realistically somewhere in between utopian and dystopian. While AI has the potential to improve areas like healthcare and everyday life, it will also bring challenges like job loss, bias, and unequal access. Utopian visions reflect our hopes for fairness and progress, while dystopian ones reveal our fears about losing control. Overall, these ideas show that AI is less about technology itself and more about how humans choose to use it, highlighting both our ambitions and concerns today.
