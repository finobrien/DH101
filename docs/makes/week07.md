# Week 7: Mapping AI Worlds

## The Artifact
![Alt text: map of AI infrastructure, labor sites, and resource locations](../assets/images/week07.png)

*Map of AI infrastructure, labor sites, and resource locations.*

## Process Notes
I made this by getting specific locations from ChatGPT and then adding them to my map in GoogleMaps. I decided which locations were important enough to include.

> **Process note:** I grouped sites by type (corporate, labor, extraction) before placing them so the map would read as a pattern.

## Reflection
I tried to map the global footprint of AI by combining corporate headquarters, data centers, labor sites, and extraction zones into one visual story. My first attempt failed because the map felt cluttered and incoherent — I had too many pins without a clear hierarchy, so the message got lost. I used ChatGPT to pull exact locations and verify place names, then I cut the list down and grouped sites into categories so the map could read as an argument instead of a database. That process taught me that AI is great at retrieval, but it doesn’t decide what should matter. The creative work was in choosing which sites to keep, how to cluster them, and how to frame the power relationships the map implies. What I learned about AI and creativity is that AI can expand the research phase quickly, but it can also overwhelm you with information if you don’t apply judgment. The final map is less about exhaustive coverage and more about showing a pattern: benefits and control are concentrated, while labor and environmental costs are dispersed. Making that pattern visible required human editing and storytelling, not just data collection.
