## Reflection
AI reshapes global geographies of power, labor, and environment by concentrating control in a few powerful places while spreading the costs everywhere. Most advanced AI systems are developed by companies like OpenAI and Google, mainly based in the US and competing with China. This centralizes decision-making power and reinforces inequalities. At the same time, much of the hidden labor that supports AI, like data labeling and content moderation, is outsourced to countries such as Kenya and India, where workers are often underpaid. AI also relies on energy, heavy data centers, and minerals mined in places like the DR of Congo, shifting environmental damage away from tech hubs. Overall, AI concentrated benefits at the top while distributing labor and environmental costs globally.
