# Week 3 – Selfie & Identity

## The Artifact


## Process Notes
How did you make this?I gave ChatGPT a picture of myself and then briefly described my appearance to have it create a selfie of me.
What tools did you use? I only used ChatGTP for this make.
What decisions did you make? I had it change my eye color just to see how AI works and what it would do with a small change. I also wanted to see if, by doing this, AI would fill in any other details about what I look like just from saying I have blue eyes.

## Reflection
In the AI-generated selfie, I can see myself in some obvious ways, like my age, hair, and the general look of a college student. The setting and overall style also match how I described myself, which makes it feel somewhat recognizable at first glance. However, a lot of what actually makes me me feels missing. The image comes across as pretty generic, almost like it could represent any student around my age rather than a specific person. What stands out is that it doesn’t really capture personality, interests, or individuality. Instead, it reduces identity to a set of surface-level traits. Because of that, the image feels less like a real representation of me and more like a general template of what an “average college student” might look like. It flattens identity into something simplified and standardized. When I changed my eye color from hazel to blue in the second version, this effect became even more noticeable. That small adjustment significantly changed how the image felt, even though nothing about my actual identity changed. It showed how easily AI can modify appearance and how much weight it gives to visual details. Eye color, which is usually just one physical trait, becomes something interchangeable, which makes identity feel more artificial or constructed. What stood out most is that the AI seems to fill in gaps based on assumptions. It produces an idealized version of a “19-year-old college student” based on patterns in its data, which ends up feeling more like a stock image than a personal selfie. Overall, it made me realize that the image is less about representing me accurately and more about reflecting the system’s idea of someone like me.

## Attribution & AI Use
- Tools used: ChatGTP.
- AI prompts (summary): “A selfie of me. I am a 19 year old college kid."
- What AI generated: A generic picture of a student wearing a backpack at a school. It could have been any student and didn’t look like me at all.
- What you changed or decided: "Change my eyes so they are blue."
