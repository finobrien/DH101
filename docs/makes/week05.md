# Week 5: GIF & Remix Culture

## The Artifact
![Week 5 GIF](./Screenshot%202026-04-29%20at%2016.07.42-1.png)

*Looping Snoopy GIF frame showing the repeated motion.*

## Process Notes
I used an AI-generated image and then looped it into a GIF. I decided everything about the loop, while AI handled the looping.

## Reflection
I tried to remix a familiar character into a short looping GIF that felt playful but also a little mechanical. My first attempt failed because the loop stuttered and the motion didn’t feel continuous — it looked like a clip restarting instead of a seamless cycle. I fixed that by trimming frames and adjusting timing so the jump and butterfly movement could repeat without a visible reset. I used AI to generate the base image of Snoopy and the scene, but the rhythm of the loop was entirely my decision. That editing step taught me that “making” a GIF isn’t just about generating the image; it’s about shaping time. I also learned something about AI and creativity: AI gives me a starting visual, but it doesn’t understand the feeling of repetition. The creativity came from deciding how long the loop should last, how fast the butterfly should move, and whether the repetition should feel playful or trapped. Because Snoopy is already part of a shared cultural library, this project is a remix in layers — the original cartoon, the AI interpretation, and my animation choices. The final GIF made me more aware of authorship as curation and timing rather than just creation.
