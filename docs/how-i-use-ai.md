# How I Use AI

## Role of AI
I use AI as a support tool rather than a replacement for my own work. I maintain control over the ideas, structure, and final decisions to ensure the content reflects my perspective. The goal is to use AI to work more efficiently while still preserving my voice and taking full responsibility for the final product.

Using AI also raises ethical concerns I have to manage directly. In this portfolio, I saw how bias can show up in outputs — for example, the “selfie” project produced a generic college-student template that flattened my identity into stereotypes. I also ran into overconfident or incomplete answers when using AI for research and analysis, which reminded me that misinformation and subtle errors can slip in fast if I accept the first response. That’s why I verify facts, cross-check locations or claims, and rewrite interpretations in my own words. Human verification matters because the work reflects my name and perspective; I’m responsible for what I publish, not the tool.

## Typical uses
- Brainstorming
- Rewording sentences
- Getting unstuck
 - Checking for clarity and flow

## What stays mine
I review and adjust everything to keep my voice, meet course expectations, and keep writing clear and accessible. If AI suggests something that feels off or inaccurate, I rewrite it in my own words.
