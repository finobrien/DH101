# How I Use AI

## Role of AI
I use AI as a support tool, not a replacement for my own work. I keep control of ideas, structure, and final decisions. The goal is to use AI to move faster without losing my voice or responsibility for the content.

## Typical uses
- Brainstorming
- Rewording sentences
- Getting unstuck
 - Checking for clarity and flow

## What stays mine
I review and adjust everything to keep my voice, meet course expectations, and keep writing clear and accessible. If AI suggests something that feels off or inaccurate, I rewrite it in my own words.
