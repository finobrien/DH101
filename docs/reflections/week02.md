# Week 2 Reflection

Reflect 2: Can AI help us define what it means to be human, or does it reduce us to patterns? AI can help us think about what it means to be human, but it can also make us feel like we are just patterns. It shows us things we might not know otherwise, like our thinking process. It also oversimplifies some things, like simple emotion. The patterns come from its understanding of everything through data that is online, which is mainly pattern-based. I think at the end of the day, it comes down to how we decide to use AI. It can help us define what it means to be human or reduce us to patterns.
