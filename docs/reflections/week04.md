# Week 4 Reflection

Reflect 4: Is AI a collaborator, tool, or plagiarist in storytelling? 
I think AI can be both a tool and a collaborator in storytelling, but it really depends on how it’s used. Most of the time, I see it more as a tool than anything else. It can help with brainstorming ideas, organizing thoughts, and getting past moments where you’re stuck. It can speed up the early stages of writing and make it easier to explore different directions for a story. However, at the end of the day, it’s still up to the person using it to decide what the story actually means and how it should come together. In some ways, AI can also feel like a collaborator because it can suggest ideas that you might not have thought of on your own. These suggestions can act as a starting point or inspiration that you then build on and shape into something more personal. It can help jump-start the creative process, especially when you’re unsure how to begin. At the same time, there are clear limits and concerns. It becomes a problem if someone uses AI to directly copy existing work or submits writing that is entirely generated without any personal input. That’s where questions about originality and academic honesty come in. Because of that, I don’t think AI is automatically plagiarism. Instead, it depends on how the writer chooses to use it and how much of their own thinking and decision-making is involved in the final product.
