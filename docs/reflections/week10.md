# Week 10 Reflection

Reflect 10: How does AI change our relationship to play, rules, and narrative? 
AI changes your relationship to play, rules, and narrative by making them more flexible and interactive instead of fixed. In traditional games or systems, rules are usually clear and consistent, and the goal is often to learn how to work within or master them. With AI, however, the experience can feel more like a collaboration than a system you are simply trying to beat. The AI can adapt to your choices in real time, which makes the interaction feel more responsive and personalized. At the same time, rules can become less obvious or more fluid. Sometimes they are not fully visible to the user, or they shift depending on how the system responds. This can make experiences feel more dynamic and engaging, but it also makes them less predictable, since you are not always sure how the system will react. Narrative also changes in important ways. Instead of following a fixed, pre-written storyline, the story can be generated or reshaped based on the player’s input. This creates a sense of shared authorship, where meaning is co-produced between the human and the AI rather than being fully controlled by one author. Overall, AI shifts play, rules, and narrative away from structured, predetermined experiences toward ones that are more personalized, evolving, and open-ended.
