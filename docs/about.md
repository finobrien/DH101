# About Me

I’m interested in how digital tools can help me tell stories and reflect on identity, culture, and everyday life. This site collects my weekly work for DH101 and shows how my thinking has grown over the semester.

## Balance
I try to keep a healthy balance between sports, friends, and the things I enjoy in my free time. School keeps me busy, but I like finding time to be outside and to work on projects that mix creativity with technology.
