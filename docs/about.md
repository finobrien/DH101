# About Me

## Snapshot
Hello! I am a college freshman and football player who values staying active and making the most of my time both on and off the field. Balancing academics and athletics has helped me develop discipline, time management skills, and a strong work ethic. Beyond sports, I am particularly interested in how digital tools can be used to tell meaningful stories and explore themes such as identity, culture, and everyday experiences. This website serves as a collection of my weekly work for DH101, highlighting not only the projects I have completed but also the growth in my thinking and perspective throughout the semester.

## Interests
- Football and Training: I am committed to football and the training it requires, focusing on discipline, strength, and continuous improvement.
- Spending Time with Friends: I enjoy building strong relationships and spending time with friends to stay balanced and connected.
- Outdoor Activities: I like being outdoors, especially fishing and snowboarding, as a way to relax and challenge myself.
- Creative Technology and Digital Storytelling: I am interested in using digital tools to tell stories and explore identity, culture, and personal experiences.

## Balance
I strive to maintain a healthy balance between sports, friendships, and the activities I enjoy in my free time. While school keeps me busy, I make it a priority to spend time outdoors and work on projects that combine creativity with technology.
