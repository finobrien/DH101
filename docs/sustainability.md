# Sustainability & Ethics

## Key takeaways
AI has a real environmental footprint. Training large models can produce significant emissions, and everyday use adds up when millions of people rely on it. The impact is not only about energy use, but also about the materials and supply chains behind hardware.

## Sources
- Strubell et al., *Energy and Policy Considerations for Deep Learning*
- ML CO2 Impact Calculator
- Public estimates of GPT-3 emissions

## My use
I use AI for schoolwork, ideas, and understanding assignments. This project reminded me to use it more intentionally, even though it is still helpful. I try to keep my prompts focused and avoid unnecessary use when I can reach the answer on my own.

## Responsibility
Individuals can be mindful, but companies have the biggest impact because they control data centers and energy use. Meaningful change depends on transparent reporting and cleaner energy, along with policies that encourage efficiency and accountability.
